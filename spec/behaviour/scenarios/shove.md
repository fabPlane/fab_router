# Scenario: shove completes routes a congested board otherwise leaves open

_generated by running a reference implementation as a sealed program twice on each board — once with
in-search shoving disabled and once with it enabled, rip-up allowed in both, the same budget in both —
and comparing the results: the per-net incomplete count before and after, `violationsAdded`, and a
fingerprint (net, position, shape) of every Pad, Pour, Fence, Rim, `held` file wire and Prior-copper
item. Budgets are stated per row. The completion targets these support are in `detailed-routing.md`;
the invariants are `rules/connectivity.md` K-17..K-19._

A **shove** displaces an already-placed free Track or Barrel to open room for another route, then
re-places it whole and design-rule-clean (`glossary`). This scenario states, per named board, the
connections that complete **only** when shoving is enabled, and the invariants that hold across the
two runs.

## Required outcome

For each board below, `route()` with in-search shoving **enabled** must leave strictly fewer
connections incomplete than the same call with it **disabled**, and must do so while:

- adding **no** Violation (`violationsAdded == 0`, R-1, K-17) in either run;
- leaving every `held`, `locked` and Prior-copper item unchanged — the fingerprint of that item set
  is identical before and after each run, and identical between the two runs (R-2, K-18); and
- never turning a connection that was complete without shoving into an incomplete one (K-19).

| Board | Budget | Incomplete-after: shoving off | shoving on | Connections that complete only with shoving | Held/locked/Prior items (unchanged) | Violations added |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `cm5-carrier.dsn` | 150 s | 5 | **0** | connections of nets `/SCL`, `/CM5/ID_SD`, `/CM5/GPIO6`, `/CM5/MOSI_GPIO20`, `/CM5/SCLK_GPIO21` | 980 | 0 |
| `Issue730-DAC2020_bm11.dsn` | 120 s | 59 | **31** | 30 of net `GND`'s connections (its incompletes fall 36 → 6) | 234 | 0 |

## Locked-channel routing (SimpleRouteJson J802)

_generated by running the SimpleRouteJson-capable reference as a sealed program on the board, counting
a connection complete when its two declared endpoints are joined and Violations under the spec's DRC
rules (`b223-j802.srj.bound.json`)._

On `b223-j802.srj.json` the board carries **locked** Prior copper of many nets — pre-existing routed
pairs and buses that sit below the default clearance among themselves but are silent against each
other (`rules/drc.md` DR-13, `formats/srj.md` J-33). Required outcome: `route()` joins 12 of the 15
declared connections by threading new Tracks and Barrels through the channels **between** that locked
Prior copper, and:

- every joined connection reaches its endpoints at clearance **at least** the applicable rule from
  the router-added copper to every other net's copper — `violationsAdded` is 0 (there are 0
  Violations before and 0 after);
- no locked Prior-copper item moves, is removed, or changes net (K-18); the router attaches its own
  copper to same-net Prior copper (K-16) but never reroutes it;
- the three connections that stay open do so for lack of a threadable channel, not because any
  Violation blocks them — the result is Violation-free throughout.

## Notes

- The "shoving off / on" columns are the reference's own raw incomplete-connection count on the
  paired runs (rip-up on in both), the apples-to-apples measure of what shoving alone changes; the
  spec-counted completion targets under the full pipeline are in `detailed-routing.md`.
- On `cm5-carrier.dsn` the 25 pre-existing different-net Violations (hand-matched differential pairs)
  are present in both runs and unchanged; shoving adds none.
- Net names are the board's own `network` names, verbatim; `/` is part of the name. Where a net has
  many connections (e.g. `GND`), the outcome names how many of its incompletes shoving resolves, not
  a single edge, because the board's own naming does not label individual edges.
- A run that reaches a lower incomplete-after by adding a Violation is not a valid shove result
  (K-17); both rows above keep `violationsAdded` at 0.
